How this was worked out, and what to keep in mind
  • Data. FEMA Disaster Declarations Summaries and Public Assistance Funded Projects Details from OpenFEMA, Tennessee rows only: —. Damage assessments load live from ArcGIS after sign-in.
  • Window. Declarations from —. The end date is the latest OpenFEMA refresh date in the files.
  • PA categories. A debris removal, B emergency protective measures, C roads and bridges, D water control facilities, E buildings and equipment, F utilities, G parks, recreational and other, Z management costs. The default is A–G; Z isn't a damage category.
  • Dollars. Federal share = federal share obligated per project. Total project cost = FEMA's estimated total cost of the project (federal and non-federal). Projects are counted in the county on the project record; when that's blank, the county comes from the applicant ID, which starts with the county FIPS code. Statewide (state agency) projects count toward declaration totals but not county measures.
  • Deobligations. The OpenFEMA project file holds each project's current obligation, not its history, so deobligations still come from the GRRF workbook and cover Categories A and B only.
  • Threshold. FEMA countywide per capita indicator for the fiscal year × 2020 census population, compared with total project cost, because FEMA's indicator measures federal and non-federal cost. Indicator values come from FEMA's published table (FY2004–FY2025), Federal Register notices (FY2002, FY2003, FY2027) and FEMA's FY2026 notice. From FY2026, the indicator applies by incident start date. FEMA used older census counts for earlier years, so older multiples are approximate.
  • Five-year chance. 1 − e−5λ, where λ is the county's PA major-disaster designations per year since 1999.
  • Escalation. An emergency declaration counts as escalated when a major declaration shares its incident ID, or covers an overlapping incident period within 120 days and shares counties.
  • $0 in these categories. Counties designated for PA with no federal share in the selected categories. Declarations under 2 years old are left out because obligations are still being written.
  • Filters. Scores, chances and dollars are worked out again on the page for the hazard and categories picked. "Emergency declaration, left out of the major" stays all hazards.
  • Damage assessments. Records marked Training, mission #999 (the training mission number) and soft-deleted records are removed. A mission is identified by its year and number and linked to a declaration through the mission number list; links marked "Possibly" are shown as unverified.
  • Linked event. When the damage assessment mission picked is linked to a major declaration in the mission number list, the hazard filter follows that declaration and the KPIs, linked event table, threshold chart and declaration views narrow to it. County scores, regions and trends stay historical for its hazard, since they rest on every declaration since 1999. Clear the mission to go back.
  • Map. County boundaries are U.S. Census county outlines (via the plotly/datasets GeoJSON).
